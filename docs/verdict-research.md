## What a careful analyst can add back to City's spending per season

Principle: only items typed HC (expenses understated) raise true spending on wages and manager costs. The OR items (830.69m tagged sponsorship, 24.5m Fordham income) do not change spending; they change whether City could legally afford it under PSR/FFP, because the revenue behind the spend was owner money, not commercial income.

Cost add-back total supported by the Decision: 66.18m over nine seasons (49.414 Fordham + 8.866 + 7.4 + 0.5).

Conservative (only items with a stated season count, no assumptions about Fordham): remuneration agreements 16.77m. Per season: Appendix 16 about 2.2m (4 seasons), Appendix 17 about 1.2m (6 seasons), Appendix 18 0.5m in one season. In a season where all overlap, about 3.4m to 3.9m; typical season about 1.2m to 3.4m. This is an average, since the Decision's per-season split is redacted/unpublished (and 8.866m is a floor).

Upper (add Fordham at even spread): 66.18m / 9 = about 7.4m per season average. Fordham alone is 49.414m, but its seasons are not public; we know it ran at least in 2014/15 and 2017/18. If Fordham ran only in, say, the last four seasons, the add-back for those seasons would be higher (about 12.4m a season for Fordham alone), but that is an illustration, not a sourced number. Do not use it without the appendices.

Practical use: model hidden cost as about 1.2m to 3.4m per season (conservative) up to about 7.4m per season (upper, flat spread), with the caveat that Fordham timing is unknown and likely back-loaded toward later seasons. The 24.5m Fordham income overstatement is a revenue-side item and should be deducted from reported revenue (about 2.7m per season if flat, unsourced spread), not added to costs. The 830.69m tagged sums are 22.5m (2009/10) rising to 134.73m (2017/18) of revenue that was ADUG funding, not commercial income.

Gaps: Fordham seasons; named individuals for Appendices 16-18; per-sponsor (Etihad/Aabar) splits; CAS primary text; Premier League's own count of upheld charges. Full Decision PDF not retrieved. ESPN and live-blog pages returned no content.
